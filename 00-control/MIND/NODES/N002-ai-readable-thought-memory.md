# Need for Structured AI-Readable Thought Memory

## The Thought
There is no durable place for fleeting thoughts, brainstorming sessions,
macro visions, or inspiration-from-videos to live. Every new AI session
starts from scratch. The user has to re-explain context constantly.
This is a critical gap — it means good ideas evaporate between sessions.

## Expanded
The problem has two dimensions:
1. CAPTURE: Thoughts are lost if not captured immediately
2. CONNECTION: Isolated thoughts have low value. Connected thoughts reveal patterns.
   Patterns reveal goals. Goals become vision.

The solution is not just a notes app — it needs:
- AI-readable structure (YAML frontmatter)
- Typed connections between thoughts (not just tags)
- Agent-driven synthesis (the AI finds links the human missed)
- Emergent vision detection (clusters of connected thoughts reveal major goals)

Historical parallel: Nikola Tesla kept a lab notebook where he cross-referenced
experiments. Individual entries were fragments. The notebook as a whole was a map
of his entire research direction. This system is that notebook, but AI-native.
