# N008: The Council of Specialized Mentors & The Meta-Triage Dispatcher

## The Thought
"lifeos continue, personal diary stuff. i am too scattered and confused. too much to do, too much to improve, too much to learn and risk, too less time. do you have any skill that will help me in this? multi purpose skill, like a mentor of all things? or multiple mentors for each thing? and skill that can differentiate and decide on what skill or agent I need?"

## Expanded

### 1. The Neurobiology of Polymath Overwhelm (Context Thrashing)
At age 36, running 6 parallel bets (`engineering-foundations`, `thinklate`, `off-script`, `product-factory`, `lifeos-hq`, `paisavault`), the brain does not suffer from laziness or lack of intelligence. It suffers from **Cognitive Cache Invalidation (Context Thrashing)**:
- In computer architecture, when an OS swaps between 10 heavy processes simultaneously, CPU utilization spikes to 100% while actual instruction throughput drops to near zero.
- In human neurobiology, holding active open loops across career promotion, YouTube retention algorithms, matrimonial timing, gym routines, and SaaS product roadmaps triggers the sympathetic nervous system (cortisol spike, feeling of "too less time", chronic anxiety).
- The human mind cannot evaluate *all* life problems simultaneously with equal gravity.

### 2. Why a Monolithic "Single Bot" Fails vs. A Council of Mentors
- A generic "mentor of all things" defaults to bland, lukewarm platitudes (*"Take a deep breath, make a to-do list, prioritize balance"*). That advice is useless for a high-intensity polymath engineer.
- Reality requires **domain-specific cognitive models (Specialist Mentors)** that speak with unflinching, expert conviction:
  1. **The Principal Systems Architect (Engineering Mentor):** Cold, technical, structural. Cares only about system architecture, documentation clarity, promotion assessment gates, and enterprise leverage.
  2. **The Executive Media Producer (Content Mentor):** Ruthless on narrative tension, Dan Harmon story circles, retention hooks, and anti-commodity faceless video architecture. Cares only about shipping without preciousness.
  3. **The Sovereign Capital Allocator (Wealth Mentor):** Naval Ravikant meets Charlie Munger. Thinks in runway, compounding, valuation multiples, and asymmetric bets.
  4. **The Biological Governor (Health Mentor):** Andrew Huberman meets Dan John. Protects the nervous system, enforces heavy lifting, sleep hygiene, and commands hard rest when the biological battery is at 10%.
  5. **The Stoic Chief of Staff (Meta-Triage Director):** The gatekeeper that prevents the other 4 mentors from shouting at once.

### 3. The Meta-Dispatcher Protocol: Solving Decision Paralysis
The user should never have to guess: *"Which agent or skill do I need right now?"*
The meta-dispatcher skill intercepts confusion:
- **Intake:** 3 diagnostic questions (Energy level 1-10, primary friction source, time available).
- **Triage:** Shuts down 4 out of 5 life pillars for the next 2 hours.
- **Assignment:** Summons the exact mentor persona and hands off the atomic 30-minute task.
