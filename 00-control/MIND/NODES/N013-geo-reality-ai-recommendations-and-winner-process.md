## Expanded
### 1. Brutal Audit: Should You Build a "GEO / AI Optimization" Product?
- **Verdict: NO for generic GEO monitoring/optimization SaaS.**
- **Empirical 2026 Reality:** The GEO tracker space is already hyper-saturated (`Otterly.ai`, `Peec AI`, `Profound`, `Scrunch`, `AthenaHQ`, plus `Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit` and `Ahrefs Brand Radar`). Building another prompt-tracker or `llms.txt` generator enters a red ocean against well-funded incumbents with zero unfair marketing-agency advantage.
- **`llms.txt` Myth vs. Reality:** Google Search Central explicitly stated Google does not use `llms.txt`; large-scale server-log studies show `GPTBot` and `PerplexityBot` request rates near zero. The *only* verified real utility of `/llms.txt` is **B2A (Business-to-Agent)** developer documentation parsed by coding agents (Cursor, Claude Code, Windsurf).

### 2. How AI Chats Actually Choose Products to Recommend in 2026
LLMs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) do not trust vendor landing pages in isolation; they synthesize **Cross-Web Consensus**:
1. **Community & Directory Ground Truth:** Organic threads on **Reddit**, **Hacker News**, **GitHub**, and **G2/Capterra** reviews heavily drive recommendations.
2. **Micro-Category Ownership:** Broad products lose to giants. Narrow emerging technical niches (where few credible tools exist yet) let a single focused utility capture the shortlist quickly.
3. **Extractable Structure & Benchmarks:** Semantic HTML, JSON-LD `SoftwareApplication` / `FAQPage` schema, Bottom-Line-Up-Front (BLUF) summaries, comparison tables, plus `/llms.txt` for developer tools.

### 3. Why Only ~1% of Apps Succeed (The Real Process vs. Noise)
1. **Distribution Inside an Existing Watering Hole:** Start where buyers already complain (`r/ClaudeAI`, `r/aws`, `Show HN`), not in an empty codebase.
2. **Hair-on-Fire Painkiller vs. Vitamin:** Solve acute technical/financial failure (token runaway, security/prompt poisoning, AWS loop bill shock, breaking schema deploys), not novelty AI wrappers.
3. **Zero-Friction Wedge $\rightarrow$ Workflow Lock-In:** 10-second browser value with no signup $\rightarrow$ embeds into CI/CD, GitHub PRs, or production proxies.
4. **4-Day Ugly Ship $\rightarrow$ 10-User Brutal Feedback Loop:** Kill or pivot within 14 days based on email/tier clicks, never 4-month silent builds.
