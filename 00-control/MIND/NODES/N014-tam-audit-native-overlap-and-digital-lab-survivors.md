## Expanded
### 1. Why All 4 Previous DevTool Calculator Ideas Were Killed
Live Nov 2026 competitor & native-feature audit proved every one of the earlier 4 point-calculators failed:
- **MCP Inspector / Firewall:** Anthropic natively shipped **Tool Search Tool** (85% token reduction), official **MCP Inspector** exists, and Invariant Labs **`mcp-scan`** + MintMCP/Lasso/Wiz own security. Tiny TAM.
- **LLM Token Cost Calculator:** Saturated with free calculators (`LiteLLM`, `Helicone`, `BenchLM`, `CostGoat`, `CloudZero`) and open proxies (`Helicone`, `Portkey`, `LiteLLM Proxy`).
- **AWS Serverless Cost/Recursion:** AWS natively built **Lambda recursive loop detection**, **EventBridge Replay**, **AWS Cost Anomaly Detection**, and **AWS Pricing Calculator**.
- **JSON-to-Zod/TS:** Dominated for free by **`transform.tools`**, **`quicktype.io`**, and inline AI IDE generation.

### 2. Strategic Clarifications Locked
- **House Repo:** **`digital-lab/`** is confirmed as the operational house.
- **True Role of ThinkLate:** Not merely AdSense vanity — **ThinkLate** (powered by `HyperFrames` + automated video pipelines) is the **production-grade marketing & ad-creative engine** for `digital-lab` products *and* a direct showcase of commercial production capability that companies pay for.

### 3. Three TAM-Verified & Non-Native Survivors (Nov 2026 Audit)
1. **Code-Maintained SaaS Launch Video & Ad Studio (`ThinkLate` / `HyperFrames` Engine -> $500B+ SaaS / $1.5K–$5K per video market):**
   - *Pain:* UI rot breaks $5K–$15K launch videos weeks after release; traditional agencies take 6–10 weeks.
   - *Competitors:* `Clueso`, `Arcade` ($42–$50/seat), `Supademo` ($38–$50/seat), `Creatomate`, `Shotstack`, `Videodeck`.
   - *Wedge:* Use `HyperFrames` + Playwright already built in `digital-lab` for rapid productized SaaS launch/ad packs or a URL-to-Motion-Teaser lead tool + showcases via our own paid/organic ads.
2. **Anti-Overlay EAA / WCAG 2.1 Compliance Scanner + Code-Patch & VPAT Generator (EU EAA Legal Mandate):**
   - *Pain:* >95% of sites fail checks; EU European Accessibility Act (EAA) makes WCAG 2.1 AA mandatory for EU-facing e-commerce/SaaS ("GDPR for accessibility"); FTC fined `accessiBe` $1M proving JS overlays don't stop lawsuits; `Lighthouse`/`Axe` only emit cryptic DOM errors, not code patches or B2B VPAT docs.
   - *Wedge:* Free Anti-Overlay EAA Risk & Code-Fix Scanner $\rightarrow$ Continuous GitHub PR/Store Monitor + Automated VPAT ($49–$149/mo). High legal fear = high marketing/ad ROI.
3. **Zero-Login Client Progress Portal for Dev/AI Agencies (`GitHub/Linear -> Magic-Link Digest`):**
   - *Pain:* Agencies burn ~20% of weekly capacity on status updates; clients refuse to log into Jira/Asana/ClickUp. Verified search demand (*"client portal for small agencies"*, *"no login project tracker"*).
   - *Competitors:* `Assembly/Copilot`, `SPP.co`, `SuiteDash` (bloated billing suites vs. lightweight $49/mo engineering status bridge).
