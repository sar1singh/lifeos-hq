# Repos as One Interconnected System

## The Thought
digital-lab, lifeos-hq, agent-os, paisavault, engineering-foundations were all
created with the same underlying intention. They are not separate projects.
They are one system. Depending on brainstorming and random vague thoughts,
the goal is to bring something actionable, useful, and valuable into the
physical world. Money is a critical factor at every stage — short, mid, long term.

## Expanded
This mental model is actually the thesis behind the entire workspace.
The repos are pillars of a single life-architecture:
- lifeos-hq runs the person
- engineering-foundations sharpens the skill
- agent-os builds the infrastructure
- digital-lab generates the output (content + products)
- paisavault tracks the financial result

When a thought emerges in one pillar, it should be able to travel
to the correct pillar automatically — via the MIND graph system.

## Open Questions
- What is the core unifying vision that all repos serve?
- When a conflict emerges between repos (e.g. lifeos says rest, digital-lab says ship), what is the tiebreaker?
