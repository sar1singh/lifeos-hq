## Expanded
### 1. SiteGPT.ai Case Study Ground Truth vs. 2026 SEO Reality
- **What actually worked for SiteGPT (Bhanu Teja, India):** Built **50+ free micro-tools** under `sitegpt.ai/tools` (PDF-to-Markdown, sitemap validators, FAQ/chatbot name generators). Those tools drove **~90% of ~50K/month organic traffic** at $0 ad spend, fueling `$10K MRR` in month 1 and `$500K+` cumulative bootstrapped revenue.
- **Crucial nuance missed in casual retelling:** He did NOT build "tools for anything and everything." Every free tool targeted the **exact ICP** (website owners & devs building RAG/AI support chatbots) who naturally upgraded to paid `SiteGPT`.
- **2026 Counter-Force (Google AI Overviews & Zero-Click Search):** In 2026, ~60%+ of generic informational queries die in AI Overviews. Generic "free AI text tools" have zero moat and burn API credits on non-paying traffic. Winners build **client-side / zero-cost interactive utilities** (calculators, validators, visualizers, analyzers) linked directly to a specific high-value paid wedge + GEO (citation-ready structure).
