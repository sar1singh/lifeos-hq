## 3. The 4-Bucket Wealth Architecture

Once the primary wealth corpus is accumulated, capital is segregated into four distinct structural buckets to eliminate sequence-of-returns risk and prevent forced selling during market drawdowns:

```text
                               TOTAL WEALTH (₹7.0 CR)
                                         │
     ┌──────────────────┬────────────────┴────────────────┬──────────────────┐
     ▼                  ▼                                 ▼                  ▼
  BUCKET 1           BUCKET 2                          BUCKET 3           BUCKET 4
 INSTANT CASH     MID-TERM SHIELD                   EQUITY ENGINE       CAPITAL ASSETS
(1-2 Yrs Living) (3-5 Yrs Stability)             (80-90% Liquid Wealth)(Home & Goals)
  ₹15L – ₹25L       ₹45L – ₹60L                      ₹3.5 Cr – ₹4.0 Cr   ₹2.0 Cr – ₹2.5 Cr
 Liquid / Arbitrage  Multi-Asset / Short Debt         Aggressive Equity    Land + Dream House
```

### Bucket 1: Instant Cash & Liquidity (1–2 Years Expenses: ₹15L – ₹25L)
- **Vehicles:** Arbitrage Funds (equity tax status, zero market volatility), Liquid Mutual Funds, High-Yield Sweep Accounts.
- **Role:** Direct monthly withdrawal tap. Even in a 2008-style market crash, you do not touch volatile assets. You draw monthly cash from Bucket 1 without stress.

### Bucket 2: Mid-Term Stability Shield (3–5 Years Expenses: ₹45L – ₹60L)
- **Vehicles:** Multi-Asset Allocation Funds, Conservative Hybrid, Short-Duration Corporate Debt / Target Maturity Funds.
- **Role:** Generates 7–8% returns with low volatility. When Bucket 1 drops below 12 months of expenses, Bucket 2 systematically refills Bucket 1.

### Bucket 3: The Compounding Growth Engine (80–90% of Liquid Assets: ₹3.5 – ₹4.0 Cr)
- **Vehicles:** Flexicap Funds, Large & Midcap, Nifty 50 Index, Emerging Smallcaps (15%), International/US Tech Index (15%).
- **Role:** Compounding at 12–14% CAGR. **This is the engine that beats 9–10% lifestyle inflation.** During equity bull markets, capital gains are harvested from Bucket 3 to replenish Buckets 1 and 2.

### Bucket 4: Dedicated Capital Asset Bucket (Dream Home: ₹2.0 – ₹2.5 Cr)
- **Vehicles:** Sinking fund transitioning into fully paid-off land and custom residential construction.
- **Role:** Physical generational security. Eliminates rent escalation and landlord friction permanently. Not counted as part of the liquid withdrawal corpus.
